KARMA — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Karma (“Karma,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a social networking and communication mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Karma, why it is used, who may process it, and how you can request access to or deletion of your information.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account and profile information: When you sign in with Google, we receive information made available through the sign-in process, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier. We also process profile information you choose to provide, such as a biography.

Content and communications: We process posts, comments, messages, stories, uploaded photos, videos, audio, and other content you choose to create or share, together with related interactions. Content you make public or send to other users may be visible to those users according to the app’s features and your choices.

Device and technical information: Our service providers and integrated SDKs may process device identifiers, IP addresses, diagnostic information, and app or advertising interaction information as needed to operate, protect, and improve their services and, where applicable, display advertisements.

Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a device registration token to deliver notifications, such as messages and service updates.

Camera, microphone, and media: If you choose to take photos, record media, upload files, or make audio/video calls, Karma may request access to your camera, microphone, or selected device media. Access is used for the feature you choose. Audio and video calls may involve transmission of real-time communication data between participants and supporting network services.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use information to authenticate users; maintain profiles; enable posts, messages, media sharing, and calls; deliver notifications; provide support; protect the app against misuse; troubleshoot technical problems; and operate advertising where enabled. We do not describe any cash-out, deposit, or withdrawal service as part of this policy.

3. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND DISCLOSURE

Karma uses Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for account, data, and notification functions; Cloudflare R2 for media storage; and Google AdMob for advertisements where enabled. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, to protect rights or safety, or when you choose to share content with other users. We do not claim that information is never shared with service providers.

4. STORAGE, SECURITY, AND RETENTION

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, subject to applicable legal requirements and actual backup and deletion processes. We do not promise a fixed deletion or backup-retention period unless it has been verified operationally.

5. YOUR CHOICES AND ACCOUNT DELETION

You may update certain profile information within the app. You may initiate account deletion using Settings > Delete Account, or request assistance at karmaapp777@gmail.com. An account-deletion request is intended to remove the account and associated personal data, including profile and user content, subject to technical limitations, data that other users have independently retained, and information that must lawfully be retained. Some data may remain temporarily in backups until those backups expire under the actual retention schedule. Contact us for details about a specific deletion request.

6. CHILDREN AND USER CONTENT

Karma contains user-generated content and communication features. Users should avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly or sharing information they do not have permission to disclose. Parents and guardians should supervise use where appropriate. Any minimum-age requirement must be stated consistently in the app and its store listing.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our services or legal requirements. We will publish the revised policy with an updated effective date.

8. CONTACT

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests: karmaapp777@gmail.com
