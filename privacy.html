Karma - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Karma ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Karma mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Information Collection and Use

While using our application, we may collect or request certain information to provide and improve our services:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your display name, email address, and profile picture URL to create and manage your account profile.
  • User Content & Data: Text messages, profile bios, K-Points balance, posts, and interaction data are securely stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Media Files: Profile pictures, user-uploaded audio tracks, and images are stored and managed via Cloudflare infrastructure.
  • Device Identifiers & Advertising Data: We collect device IDs, IP addresses, and ad interaction data via Google AdMob to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) collects device registration tokens to deliver real-time notifications for messages, rewards, and system updates.

2. App Permissions and Hardware Access

To support key interactive features, Karma requests access to specific device capabilities:

  • Camera Permission: Required when you choose to capture photos or record video for your profile or social posts.
  • Microphone Permission: Required to capture audio during Voice and Video Calling features, as well as for user-recorded audio content.
  • Storage / Photos & Media Access: Required to let you select and upload image and audio files from your device.

3. Use of Data

Karma uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our service (such as user authentication, posts, audio streaming, and audio/video calls).
  • To facilitate the K-Points reward system and monetization requests.
  • To serve ads and generate revenue via Google AdMob.
  • To send push notifications and account updates.
  • To ensure system security, prevent fraud, and manage user support.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Firebase & Google Sign-In: Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, and FCM Push Notifications.
  • Google AdMob: Advertisements, analytics, and monetization performance tracking.
  • Cloudflare: Secure media, image, and file hosting infrastructure.

5. Account Deletion & Data Retention

We respect your right to control your personal data. You can delete your account at any time directly within the app via Settings > Delete Account, or by contacting customer support.

  • Data Deleted Upon Account Removal: When your account is deleted, your profile information, display name, email address, bio, profile photo, K-Points balance, and user-generated posts/media will be permanently deleted from our primary servers.
  • Data Retained: For legal, regulatory, anti-fraud, and financial accounting purposes (e.g., payout/withdrawal history logs), transaction records related to monetization requests may be retained in anonymized backups for up to 90 days before permanent purging.

6. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We use industry-standard security measures (including secure cloud databases like Firebase/Firestore and Cloudflare storage) to protect your personal information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or account deletion requests, please contact us by email: karmaapp777@gmail.com
