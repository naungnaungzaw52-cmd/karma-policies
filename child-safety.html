Karma App – Child Safety Standards Policy

At Karma, we are committed to providing a safe and secure social networking environment for all users. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

1. Prohibited Content and Behavior

Karma strictly prohibits:

  • Any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Creating, uploading, sharing, distributing, or promoting CSAM.
  • Sexual grooming, solicitation, or exploitation of minors.
  • Any content or behavior that endangers the safety and well-being of children.

Violations of these standards may result in immediate content removal, account suspension, permanent bans, and reporting to appropriate authorities.

2. In-App Reporting and Content Moderation

Karma provides an in-app reporting feature that allows users to report inappropriate content, harmful behavior, and accounts that violate our community safety standards.

Users may also report child safety concerns directly to our designated safety contact.

  • Moderation Review: Our moderation team aims to review reported content and accounts within 24 hours.
  • Enforcement Actions: When violations involving CSAE or CSAM are identified, we take appropriate action, including removing prohibited content and suspending or permanently banning responsible accounts.

3. Child Safety and Law Enforcement Cooperation

Karma is committed to complying with applicable child protection and online safety laws.

When CSAM or CSAE is identified, we take appropriate steps to report such incidents to relevant authorities and designated organizations, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), where required or appropriate under applicable law.

We cooperate with lawful investigations and requests from relevant authorities.

4. Designated Child Safety Contact

For questions, concerns, or urgent reports related to child safety on Karma, please contact our designated child safety representative.
