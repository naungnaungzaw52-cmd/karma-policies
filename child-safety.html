Karma App - Child Safety Standards Policy

At Karma, we are fully committed to creating a safe and secure environment for all our users. We strictly prohibit any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE) within our platform.

1. In-App Reporting & Content Moderation

We provide robust in-app mechanisms that allow users to immediately report any inappropriate content, harmful interactions, or accounts that violate community safety rules.

  • Moderation Review: Our moderation team reviews all flagged reports within 24 hours.
  • Enforcement Actions: Any content found to involve CSAM or CSAE is immediately removed, and the associated user account is permanently suspended or banned.

2. Law Enforcement Cooperation

Karma adheres strictly to international child protection laws. Any detected instances of CSAM/CSAE will be reported to appropriate law enforcement agencies and organizations such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
